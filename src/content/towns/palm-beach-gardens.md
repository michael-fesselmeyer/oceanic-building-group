Palm Beach Gardens is a city in northern Palm Beach County and the home of PGA National. Where a lot is not on the beach, the coastal construction control line does not come into play, and the neighborhood matters more.

## Communities with their own reviewers

Many Palm Beach Gardens neighborhoods are golf communities and gated communities with an association and an architectural review board. Their review is separate from the City's, and association approval is often needed before a permit application makes sense. We plan for that approval first, since a design that satisfies the building code can still be sent back on exterior color, roofline or setbacks.

## Permits through the City

The City of Palm Beach Gardens runs its own permitting and takes applications through an online portal. For an addition or remodel, the City's plan review and the association's review run one after the other, so the schedule we give a client has both in it.

## Flood and wind still apply

Not being on the beach does not remove flood rules. Lots near canals and lakes may sit in a mapped flood zone, where the City's floodplain rules apply, including the National Flood Insurance Program's rule that an improvement costing half or more of a building's market value brings the whole building up to current standards. And the Florida Building Code's wind requirements apply across the county, so structure, roof connections and openings are designed to the wind speed for the address.
