Hobe Sound is in Martin County, just across the line from Palm Beach County, and is the home of Jonathan Dickinson State Park. It is on the mainland side of the Intracoastal Waterway; the barrier island across the water is Jupiter Island.

## Two jurisdictions to keep straight

Mainland Hobe Sound's building permits are handled by Martin County's Building Department, which asks applicants to register and apply through its online portal. Jupiter Island is a separate town with a Building Department of its own. Knowing which side of the water a lot is on tells us whose rules, forms and inspectors apply, and that is the first thing we establish when someone asks about a site here.

## County rules, not Palm Beach County's

Martin County is a different county from Palm Beach County, with its own permit system and its own flood and zoning rules. Our experience on a Palm Beach County project does not transfer automatically, so we read Martin County's requirements for each project. Along the Atlantic, the state's coastal construction control line also runs through Martin County, and construction seaward of it needs a state permit unless it is exempt.

## Large sites

On an estate-scale site there is room for a main house, guest quarters, a pool and outbuildings. That gives a design room to work, and it also means more to coordinate: utility connections, drainage across a bigger site, and the order in which the pieces are built.
