Wellington is a village in western Palm Beach County, and its identity is horses. The Village hosts the Winter Equestrian Festival, the Global Dressage Festival and major polo tournaments, and its Equestrian Preserve Area covers approximately 9,200 acres, almost 30 percent of Wellington's developable land.

## Building for horses and people

Work in Wellington often means a barn, an arena or a stable alongside a house. Each of these is a building with a roof, a structure and a permit, and they are designed with the horse in mind as well as the code: ventilation and airflow, floors and drainage that are safe underfoot, and wash areas, tack rooms and storage that suit how the barn is used. The Equestrian Preserve Area has its own planning rules from the Village, so the use of a parcel is confirmed before design begins.

## The Village's building department

Wellington has its own Building Department, and plan review is submitted through the Village's online self-service portal. We confirm current submittal requirements with the Village at the start of each project.

## Inland, but not out of the wind

Wellington is well inland, so the coastal construction control line is not a factor here. The Florida Building Code's wind requirements still apply, and a large open building such as a barn or arena is a significant wind-load design problem in its own right. Flood maps matter too in a place with canals and drainage.
