Jupiter is a Palm Beach County town built around its water. The Loxahatchee River and the Intracoastal Waterway reach the Atlantic at Jupiter Inlet, and the Jupiter Inlet Lighthouse stands at the inlet's edge. A lot here may front the river, the Intracoastal, a canal or the ocean, and which one it is shapes almost every decision about the house.

## Water decides the design

The Federal Emergency Management Agency's flood maps are the starting point for any lot near the water. Under the National Flood Insurance Program, each participating community, Jupiter included, adopts its own floodplain rules, and those rules require the lowest occupied floor of a new or substantially improved building to sit a set height above the flood elevation on the maps. An elevation certificate documents it. We read the flood zone before drawing anything, because it fixes the floor height, the foundation and how a garage or ground-level room can be used.

A beachfront lot adds another layer. Florida's Department of Environmental Protection draws a coastal construction control line along the state's sandy beaches, and construction seaward of that line needs a state permit unless it is exempt. It is a permit boundary, not a setback, and a parcel's position relative to it is worth knowing before a purchase or a design.

## Renovations near the water

A renovation can trigger the same flood rules as a new house. Under the National Flood Insurance Program's substantial improvement rule, an addition or remodel that costs half or more of the building's market value brings the whole building up to the standard for new construction, and a community may set a stricter threshold. For an older Jupiter home on the water, that threshold is worth checking before the scope is set.

## Permits

The Town of Jupiter uses an online Community Development System portal, where an applicant can check plan review status and comments and schedule inspections. We confirm the current requirements with the Town at the start of each project rather than assume them.
