The Town of Jupiter Inlet Colony is a small town in northern Palm Beach County, with its own Town Hall and Town Code. For a builder that means the Town itself, not the county, is the contact for permits, rules and inspections.

## One small building department

The Town runs its own Building Department, which issues permits and performs inspections, and it asks contractors to register with the Town before work begins. Applications are taken in person, by email or through an online permit system, and the Town does not accept the county-wide universal permit form. Inspections are scheduled ahead, and same-day inspections are not offered. These are the details that decide how a schedule is set, so we check them with the Town at the start of a job.

## Building for the coast

Homes here are built to the Florida Building Code's wind provisions for coastal sites. In a wind-borne debris region, the code calls for protected openings: impact-rated windows and doors or approved shutters. The flood map for the specific lot sets the minimum floor height, and Palm Beach County's floodplain rules apply the same standard to a renovation that costs half or more of the building's market value.
