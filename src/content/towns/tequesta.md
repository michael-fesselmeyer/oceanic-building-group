The Village of Tequesta lies along the Loxahatchee River in northern Palm Beach County, next to Jupiter. The Village publishes flood information for residents and builders, and a lot near the river is the kind most likely to fall in a mapped flood zone.

## A floodplain permit before the building permit

Tequesta is specific about flood zones. If a lot is in a mapped Special Flood Hazard Area, a Floodplain Development Permit is required before the building permit can be issued, and an elevation certificate is required for all permitted work in that area. That means the survey and the elevation work come early in a project's schedule, not late.

The Village also applies the half-of-market-value rule to existing buildings: when repairs or improvements equal or exceed 50 percent of the building's market value, the building must be brought up to the current flood standards. If you own an older riverfront house and are planning an addition, this is the first number to look at.

## The Village's own building department

Tequesta has its own Building Department, with inspections scheduled through its online portal or by phone. A builder here deals with the Village directly, which keeps questions about plan review and inspections close to the people who answer them.

## Wind

As across the coast, the Florida Building Code's wind requirements govern the structure and the openings. The design wind speed and any wind-borne debris requirements are confirmed for each address during plan review.
