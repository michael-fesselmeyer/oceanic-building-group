Oceanic Building Group builds homes on speculation and is always looking for its next project. If you own land, or have a property in mind, we are open to a conversation.

## Land, outright or as a joint venture

We will buy land outright, and we are also happy to discuss a joint venture.

## Where we look

We favor properties near the water. For any site we weigh the average sale prices in the area and how long homes there take to sell.

## Building for the buyer

We use quality finishes to attract the high-end buyer. Pricing and marketing the home well is key to a quick sale, so we plan both from the start.

## How long it takes

Once approvals are in, a typical build takes 11 to 12 months, depending on square footage. One example from our records is a home built in 8 months that sold 55 days after it was finished.

Every property is different, and we make no promises about sale price, timing or return. Each one is discussed on its own merits.
