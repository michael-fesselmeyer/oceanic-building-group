This home was built with a large family in mind, and its plan makes the most of the square footage on a quarter-acre lot. It has five bedrooms, four and a half baths, a formal dining room, two large family rooms and a living room. A cultured stone that looks like wood accents the exterior.

From the front door you can see through to the heated saltwater pool and spa. The kitchen cabinets are Italian laminate lacquer, dark wood-look on the lower run and bright white gloss above. The quartz countertop waterfalls over the large island, and the kitchen includes a wine refrigerator and a clear ice machine.

The house was built in eight months.
