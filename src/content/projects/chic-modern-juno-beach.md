This modern home was built a few steps from Juno Beach. Its clean lines are drawn to take in the views of the Atlantic Ocean, and an elevator reaches the rooftop terrace for a longer look.

The structure uses the Hambro system, which combines steel joists with a transversely reinforced concrete slab. The terrace is decked in Ipe, a South American hardwood chosen for its looks and its long life.
