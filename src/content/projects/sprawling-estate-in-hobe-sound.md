This private residence is set back on 11.5 acres of native vegetation. The exterior is solid concrete: poured masonry block walls and a roof structure of 5-inch solid concrete, covered with aluminum standing seam metal roofing for a longer service life. Hurricane doors and windows complete the envelope.

The main residence is 9,500 square feet, with a 1,328 square foot attached garage. Three other buildings share the grounds: a 1,131 square foot guest house, a 500 square foot tennis house beside the private court, and a 1,100 square foot boat house. The boat ramp and boat storage give access to a slalom water ski course.

Inside, the main house has hand-scraped walnut floors, spa showers with body sprays and sauna features, and a private movie theater with a ceiling that shows the constellations. A Crestron system controls the house from on site or remotely. Landscaping was started at the beginning of the project so the trees and plantings were mature when the buildings were finished, and the entrance is gated with security cameras.
