This custom barn in Wellington is a 20,000 square foot facility with thirty-two stalls, including two stallion stalls, and eight wash stalls. The grooms' quarters cover 2,000 square feet with four bedrooms and four baths, so the horses always have someone close by. There is also a private owner's lounge and meeting area, and a custom formulated sand arena.

The building was designed to need little upkeep. It has color-infused stucco inside and out, Azek trim and shutters, and core-filled block walls with Icynene insulation. Impact windows and doors protect it in hurricane season. Inside, a tongue and groove barrel ceiling with cove lighting is one of the finer details.
