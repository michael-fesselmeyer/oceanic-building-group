This home sits on the Loxahatchee River and was planned around the view. Every bedroom in the main house looks onto the water, and the balconies and large lanai face the river and the infinity edge pool.

The open kitchen has two large islands topped with blue calcite. Custom molded ceilings run through the main rooms, and large sliding glass doors across the back of the house open the living areas to the breeze. A Control4 system programs the LED lighting and the surround sound.

The property also includes a guest house with a kitchenette.
