This home was built along the beach path for a buyer who wanted a relaxed, coastal feel. Lots in the area are limited in size, so the plan had to work to fit both indoor and outdoor living. The large covered loggia has a pecky cypress ceiling and an outdoor summer kitchen for entertaining.

The house has two stories. The primary suite is on the first floor, with a marble bathroom and separate custom closets and vanities. The open kitchen has quartz countertops and high-end appliances, and custom Chippendale wood railings finish the style.

It took 11 months to build.
