This home was built to bring the Key West feel to Jupiter Inlet Colony, close to the beach walkway. It is a two-story house with a large family room and an open floor plan.

Inside the front door the view runs through to the backyard, with its pool and spa. A cathedral ceiling with open trusses gives the main room its character. The second floor has two bedrooms and a theater-style living room.

It took 11 months to build.
