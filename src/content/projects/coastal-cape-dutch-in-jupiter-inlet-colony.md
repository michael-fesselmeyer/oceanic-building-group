This was a complete renovation. The original house had 2,732 square feet of living space and the finished home has 3,700, with four bedrooms and four and a half baths. The design takes its cue from Cape Dutch architecture and the West Indies beach style.

The front of the house has a whimsical gable and a white facade, set off by gas lanterns, Sylvester palms, Bermuda grass and climbing Clusia hedges. At the back, floor-to-ceiling doors open to the yard, pool and putting green.

The homeowner designed the interior around open living areas. They have nickel joint shiplap, coffered ceilings, a whitewashed pecky cypress fireplace and wide plank white oak floors. Custom staircase railings, lighting, inset cabinetry and built-ins, and a French range add to the finish. Calacatta Gold marble covers the kitchen island and backsplash and appears in the primary bath.
