Ashley Manfred designs homes that feel warm and lived in. Each one starts with the client: how
they spend their days, what they already own and love, and what they want the rooms to feel like.

Her rooms are built up in layers. Vintage pieces sit beside custom upholstery, and color and
pattern are used with confidence, from printed fabrics to papered walls.

She studied at the University of Michigan and taught in New York City before turning to interior
design. Today she works with clients across the Northeast, in Florida and in the Midwest.
